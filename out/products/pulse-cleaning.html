Why Pulsed Laser Cleaning?

Non-Contact Cleaning

Laser beam removes contaminants without touching the substrate. No mechanical stress, no surface damage.

No Chemicals Required

Environmentally friendly process. No solvents, no abrasives, no hazardous waste disposal needed.

Precision Control

Adjustable power and frequency for different materials. Clean rust, paint, oil, oxide layers precisely.

High Efficiency

Up to 15 m²/h cleaning speed at 500W. 5-10x faster than traditional sandblasting or chemical methods.

Air-Cooled Design

No external chiller needed. Compact, portable, and ready to use in any workshop environment.

Wide Applications

Rust removal, paint stripping, weld pre-treatment, mold cleaning, heritage restoration, and more.
