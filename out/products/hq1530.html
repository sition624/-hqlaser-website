Core Components

Raycus / IPG / MAX Laser Source

Premium fiber laser generator with 100,000+ hours lifespan. Electro-optical conversion efficiency up to 30%.

CypCut BC100S Control System

Industry-leading CNC controller with auto-nesting, edge detection, and path optimization. Supports DXF/PLT/AI formats.

Single Platform Work Table

Heavy-duty steel welded platform with blade table. High rigidity and stability for precise cutting. Easy slag cleaning and maintenance.

PRECITEC / Raytools Auto-Focus Head

Capacitive height sensing with auto-focus. Maintains optimal focal position for consistent cut quality at high speed.

HIWIN / Yaskawa Servo Drive

Japanese precision AC servo motors. Smooth acceleration up to 1.5G, ensuring high dynamic cutting performance.

Raytools Cutting Head

QBH connector, collimating/focusing lens protection. Compatible with Raycus, IPG, MAX sources.
