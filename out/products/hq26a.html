Key Highlights

Raycus Fiber Laser Source

Trusted Chinese laser brand. 100,000+ hours diode lifespan, 30%+ electro-optical efficiency. High beam quality for stable, consistent welding.

Air-Cooled for Reliability

No external chiller needed. Built-in air cooling system. IP65 protection rating. Operates in -10~40°C environments.

Precise Welds. Clean Finish.

Minimal heat affected zone, no discoloration, smooth and clean weld seam. 3-5x faster than TIG welding.

Auto Wire Feeder

Integrated automatic wire feeding system. Supports 0.8-1.6mm wire diameters for versatile welding applications.

Intelligent Monitoring System

Real-time temperature monitoring, fault alarm with error codes, safety interlock, and welding parameter data recording.

Portable & Lightweight

Compact design with caster wheels and carry handles. One person can move it. Setup in 5 minutes.
